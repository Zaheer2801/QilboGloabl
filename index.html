Why Qilbo exists

"I didn't build Qilbo in an office. I built it behind my own counter, because the numbers were too many to watch by hand."

One night I checked my price book: only 306 of 2,077 items had a cost on file. A Don Julio was ringing at a 27% margin. A Crown Royal rang up at $0.00. I'd paid full price on a case when the deal was sitting right there. Nobody was lazy. There was just no one checking every night. Now Qilbo does, and every check on this page started as a real mistake in my store.

Security

Your data, locked down.

Six promises we keep for every store.

Read-only

Qilbo never changes your register, never places orders and never moves money. It suggests; you decide.

Encrypted

Your data travels over HTTPS and is stored encrypted on Cloudflare's infrastructure.

No passwords kept

Gmail connects through Google's own sign-in, so Qilbo never sees your password. Distributor passwords are never stored.

Only what it needs

Qilbo only picks up emails from your POS report sender and your distributors. The rest of your inbox is left alone.

Every store has its own ID

Each store's data is tied to its own store ID and kept separate from every other store.

Not used to train AI

Invoices are read with a paid AI service whose terms don't allow training on your data. Export or delete everything any time.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need to change my POS?

No. Qilbo works next to the register you have. It reads your daily sales reports and invoices, and gives you a list of changes to make yourself.

Is it only for liquor stores?

No. Qilbo started in a liquor store, and the same checks work for convenience stores and smoke and vape shops: packs and singles, broken cases, cartons, flavors, lottery and bill pay kept out of your margins, and manufacturer programs.

Which POS systems work?

We start with NRS daily reports. Most other registers can export sales and a price book as a CSV file, which Qilbo can read.

How long does setup take?

About an hour with us for the first connection. Qilbo then loads your past invoices so your costs are filled in, and you get your first morning brief the next day.

Does the AI change prices on its own?

Never. Qilbo suggests and explains. You approve, and the change is made by you or your staff at the register.

Is my data safe?

Each store's data is kept separate and is never shared with other stores. You can export it, or ask us to delete it, at any time.

I have more than one store.

Each store gets its own brief and settings, and you see them all in one place. Talk to us about multi-store pricing.
