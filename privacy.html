Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

The short version: Qilbo reads only the store data it needs to check your prices, costs and orders. It never changes your register, never sells your data, and never uses it to train AI. You can export or delete your data at any time.

Who we are

Qilbo ("we", "us") makes software that helps independent liquor stores, convenience stores and smoke shops check their prices, costs, deals and reorders. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Store reports. Daily sales and price book reports from your point-of-sale system, sent by email or uploaded as files.
  • Invoices. Distributor invoices you forward, upload or photograph, including items, case sizes, costs and deals.
  • Messages you send Qilbo. Questions and approvals sent through Telegram or the Qilbo app, and customer requests you log.
  • Account details. Your name, store name, phone or WhatsApp number, city, and the settings you choose, like margin targets.
  • Basic usage data. Technical logs such as time of access and errors, used to keep the service working and secure.

We do not collect card payments from your customers, customer identities, or your passwords for distributor websites.

Gmail access

If you connect Gmail, Qilbo reads only messages from senders you approve: your POS report sender and your distributors. The rest of your inbox is left alone. Qilbo does not send email from your account, delete your email, or read personal mail.

Qilbo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train general AI or machine learning models, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising.

How we use your data

  • To read your reports and invoices and keep your costs up to date.
  • To run the nightly checks and prepare your morning brief and alerts.
  • To answer your questions about your own store.
  • To support you, fix problems and keep the service secure.

AI processing

Qilbo uses a paid AI service to read invoices and answer questions. Under that service's paid terms, your data is not used to train its models. Only the part of your data needed for a task is sent.

Where it's stored and who can see it

Your data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Each store's data is tied to its own store ID and kept separate from every other store. Only you, the people you add to your store, and Qilbo staff who need it to support you can see it.

We share data only with the service providers that run Qilbo (hosting, AI processing and messaging), only for that purpose, or when the law requires it. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active so Qilbo can compare prices and costs over time. When you close your account, we delete your store's data within 30 days, except where we must keep records by law.

Your choices

  • Export your data as a spreadsheet at any time.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data.
  • Disconnect Gmail or any other connection at any time from your Google account or from Qilbo.

Children

Qilbo is a business tool for store owners and their staff. It is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests: hello@qilbo.com, or use the contact form.
